About Us

Survey, Mapping & Planning in Raniganj

Nag & Associates works from Raniganj, in the coal belt of Paschim Bardhaman, West Bengal. We survey mining and industrial projects, roads, railways, private land and buildings.

Our services include fieldwork, digital drafting, plan preparation for government approvals, and large-format printing. Our sister concern, Aadrita Buildcon LLP, builds commercial and residential buildings, so clients can get both survey and construction done by one group.

  • Land & mines surveys for industrial and mining projects
  • Mouza plot survey & layout using Total Station and GPS
  • Plans for ADDA NOC, AMC building approval, B.L. & L.R.O. conversion
  • Engineering drawing printing up to A0+ in-house
