About us

Precise measurement is where every good project begins.

Nag & Associates Survey Consultancy is a land and engineering survey firm based in Raniganj, Paschim Bardhaman, West Bengal. We provide topographical, industrial, infrastructure and railway survey and mapping solutions for landowners, builders, industries and government projects.

We combine field experience with modern equipment, including Total Station, DGPS and drones, to deliver reliable data, clear maps and complete documentation for approvals such as building plans and ADDA land conversion NOC.

Together with our sister concern, Aadrita Buildcon LLP, we support your project from the first survey to the finished building.

Our services

Survey & mapping services

From a single plot to kilometres of road and rail, we survey it accurately.

Topographical Survey

Detailed mapping of ground levels, natural features and existing structures for planning and design.

Contour Survey & Mapping

Accurate contour plans for earthwork, drainage design, site grading and levelling.

Industrial Survey

Plant layout, alignment and as-built surveys for factories, mines and industrial sites.

Boundary Survey & Demarcation

Precise identification and marking of property boundaries to prevent and resolve disputes.

Railway Track Survey

Track alignment, level and curve surveys for new lines, sidings and maintenance works.

Roads & Infrastructure Survey

Route alignment, cross-sections and quantity surveys for roads, bridges and utilities.

Drone Survey & 3D Mapping

Aerial imaging, orthomosaics and 3D terrain models for large or hard-to-reach areas.

Building Plan Approval (AMC)

Survey, drawings and documentation support for building plan approval from the Asansol Municipal Corporation.

ADDA Land Conversion NOC

End-to-end assistance with surveys and paperwork for land conversion NOC from ADDA.
