A Journey Built on Experience, Precision and Trust

Our journey began in 2002 with the establishment of Nag & Associates by Late Sri Dilip Kumar Nag, popularly known as Nag Babu, a former Survey Superintendent of Eastern Coalfields Limited (ECL).

Drawing upon his professional experience in surveying and land-related technical work, he established an organisation dedicated to accurate measurement, reliable documentation and professional service.

In 2012, Amit Kumar Nag joined Nag & Associates as a Consulting Civil Engineer and Surveyor. With his technical background and professional experience, he continued the legacy while gradually expanding the scope of activities into civil engineering, building planning, advanced surveying, statutory approval consultancy and technical documentation.

With the changing requirements of the surveying and construction industry, the business expanded its operations through Topogrid Consultants, a GST-registered unit of Aadrita Buildcon LLP. Topogrid Consultants operates in the same professional field as Nag & Associates, providing the same range of surveying, civil engineering, planning and consultancy services.

Alongside these professional activities, Aadrita Buildcon LLP was developed as a dedicated construction and real estate development business, extending the organisation's capabilities into residential and commercial construction, turnkey projects, renovation and property development.

Today, these business identities represent different business establishments working across complementary areas of the land, engineering and construction sectors.

NAG & ASSOCIATES

Established 2002

Land Surveying | Civil Engineering | Planning & Consultancy

Nag & Associates is the founding professional establishment of our organisation, established in 2002 by Late Sri Dilip Kumar Nag.

With a foundation in professional surveying and land-related technical services, Nag & Associates has developed its activities across land measurement, topographical surveying, civil engineering, building planning, approval consultancy and technical documentation.

Since 2012, under the professional association of Amit Kumar Nag, the organisation has continued to expand its technical capabilities and service portfolio.

Our focus is to provide reliable surveying and engineering solutions for individual landowners, property developers, commercial establishments, industrial organisations and infrastructure-related projects.

Nag & Associates represents our original professional identity and the experience upon which our subsequent business activities have developed.

TOPOGRID CONSULTANTS

A Unit of Aadrita Buildcon LLP GST-Registered Business Unit

Land Surveying | Civil Engineering | Planning & Consultancy

Topogrid Consultants is a GST-registered business unit of Aadrita Buildcon LLP, operating in the same professional field as Nag & Associates.

Developed as an extension of the surveying and engineering activities, Topogrid Consultants provides the same broad range of professional services, including land surveying, topographical surveys, DGPS and GNSS surveys, Total Station surveys, drone mapping, GIS, civil engineering consultancy, building planning and statutory documentation assistance.

Topogrid Consultants represents an additional registered business identity through which professional assignments and client engagements are undertaken.

Both Nag & Associates and Topogrid Consultants maintain the same nature of business activities and service capabilities.

The applicable business identity for an assignment, quotation, work order and invoice is determined by the relevant engagement and billing requirements.

From Concept to Completion.

Aadrita Buildcon LLP is our dedicated building construction and real estate development business.
