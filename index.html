About the firm

A partner who knows your business, not just your books.

Nag & Associates is a professional services firm offering assurance, taxation and advisory services. We work closely with our clients so that compliance never becomes a distraction from running the business.

Every engagement is led by a partner, backed by a team that understands the regulatory landscape and uses modern tools to deliver accurate work on time.

  • Partner-led engagements
  • Transparent, fixed-scope fees
  • Timely filings and proactive reminders
  • Strict confidentiality of client data

What we do

Services

End-to-end support across the financial life of your business.

Audit & Assurance

Statutory audits, tax audits, internal audits, stock and concurrent audits with clear, actionable reporting.

Direct Taxation

Income tax planning and returns, TDS compliance, assessments, appeals and representation before authorities.

GST & Indirect Tax

GST registration, monthly and annual returns, reconciliations, refunds, audits and advisory on transactions.

Company Law & ROC

Company and LLP incorporation, annual ROC filings, secretarial compliance and restructuring support.

Startup Advisory

Entity structuring, Startup India registration, valuations, fundraising support and investor-ready financials.

Accounting & Virtual CFO

Bookkeeping, payroll, MIS reports, budgeting and cash-flow planning so you always know where you stand.
