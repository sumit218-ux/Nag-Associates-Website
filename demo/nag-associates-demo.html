About us

Precise measurement is where every good project begins.

Nag & Associates Survey Consultancy is a digital survey and printing firm based at Tantia Complex, N.S.B. Road, Raniganj. We specialise in all types of engineering survey, land and mines survey, and topographic, contour, quantity and mouza plot surveys for landowners, builders, mining and industrial projects, and government works.

We combine field experience with modern equipment, including Total Station, GPS/DGPS and drones, and prepare the plans and documents you need: building and site plans, registration and assessment plans, and conversion (Bastu) plans for ADDA NOC and BL & LRO. Our in-house printing desk handles engineering drawings, A0+ prints, scanning, jumbo photocopies, ammonia prints and mouza sheets.

Together with our sister concern, Aadrita Buildcon LLP, we support your project from the first survey to the finished building.

Our services

Survey & mapping services

Specialists in all types of engineering survey, from a single mouza plot to mines, roads and railway lines, using Total Station & GPS.

Engineering Survey

All types of engineering surveys for design, setting out and construction of civil works.

Land & Mines Survey

Land measurement and mine surveys, including pit, dump and lease-area mapping.

Topographic Survey

Detailed mapping of ground levels, natural features and existing structures for planning and design.

Contour Survey & Mapping

Accurate contour plans for earthwork, drainage design, site grading and levelling.

Mining & Industrial Projects

Plant layout, alignment and as-built surveys for mining and industrial projects.

Quantity Survey

Volume and quantity calculations for earthwork, stockpiles, excavation and billing.

Mouza Plot Survey & Layout

Plot surveys and layouts with Total Station & GPS, matched against mouza maps and records.

Boundary Survey & Demarcation

Precise identification and marking of property boundaries to prevent and resolve disputes.

Railway Track Survey

Track alignment, level and curve surveys for new lines, sidings and maintenance works.

Roads & Infrastructure Survey

Route alignment, cross-sections and longitudinal sections for roads, bridges and utilities.

Drone Survey & 3D Mapping

Aerial imaging, orthomosaics and 3D terrain models for large or hard-to-reach areas.
